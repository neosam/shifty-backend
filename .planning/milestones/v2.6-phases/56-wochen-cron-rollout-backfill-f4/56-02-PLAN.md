<objective>
Add the F4 core business logic to the existing RebookingReconciliationService: `run_for_week` (process one ISO week for one capped SalesPerson) and `backfill_range` (iterate historical weeks with a dry-run preview). Both reuse the Phase-55 internal `rebook()` pair-write-path and the Phase-54 UNIQUE index for idempotency.

Purpose: This is the trigger-agnostic engine the scheduler (Plan 03) and the backfill endpoint (Plan 04) both call. Keeping the excess computation and the write in one service method (single write-path, Pitfall 15) guarantees the cron, the backfill, and manual rebooking all produce identical rebooking pairs. The F4-Self-Guard (per D-06) lives here on the write side: the cron only writes for weeks >= active_from; the read side is already neutral from Phase 55.

Output: trait extensions (`run_for_week`, `backfill_range`, `RunForWeekOutcome`, `SkipReason`, `BackfillPreviewItem`) plus impl and two integration test modules covering REB-AUTO-01/03, D-02, D-04, and the dry-run/apply split (REB-AUTO-04 core).
</objective>
