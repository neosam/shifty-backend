<objective>
Add the frontend Admin surface for F4: a Settings card that edits the Stichtag toggle (`voluntary_rebooking_auto_active_from`) and drives the backfill endpoint with a dry-run preview table, plus the `/admin/rebooking` Dioxus proxy and de/en/cs labels.

Purpose: REB-AUTO-04 needs an operator UI (no CLI). REB-AUTO-02 needs the Stichtag editable. Both mirror shipped analogs exactly — the Card-2b shortday activation-date editor and the pdf_export_config card — so this is glue and rendering, not new UX invention. The FE renders the backend-computed `BackfillPreviewTO` without arithmetic (Fat Backend, Thin Client). The proxy entry is the classic Pitfall-5 trap: without it the backfill POST 404s in `dx serve`.

Output: Dioxus.toml proxy, api.rs backfill POST + toggle getters/setters, loader wrappers, the Settings admin card, a thin `state/rebooking.rs` mapper for the preview TO (if needed), and i18n keys in all three locales.
</objective>
