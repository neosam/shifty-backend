in der Codebase noch nicht vorhanden).

|----------|-------------|
| client→REST→Service | HR-Aufrufer initiiert Approve/Reject; State-Wechsel ist privilegierte Operation |
| Service→DAO→SQLite | State-Transition muss atomar/race-frei sein |

## STRIDE Threat Register (ASVS L1, block-on: high)

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-55-01 | Tampering | update_state (Double-Approve-Race) | high | mitigate | state-conditional UPDATE `WHERE state='pending'` + `rows_affected()==1`-Guard; Race-Verlierer bekommt Conflict (→ HTTP 409). Test double_approve_second_is_conflict. |
| T-55-02 | Elevation of Privilege | update_state / find_pending (Non-HR) | high | mitigate | `check_permission(HR_PRIVILEGE, …)` an ERSTER Stelle jeder Service-Methode, vor Datenabruf. Test update_state_is_hr_gated. |
| T-55-03 | Tampering | SQL-Injection in neuen Queries | high | mitigate | `sqlx::query!` parametrisiert + compile-time-checked (`cargo sqlx prepare`) |
| T-55-04 | Repudiation | Reject spurlos | low | accept | Rejected-Batch bleibt persistent (D-55-04, Audit-Trail); kein Delete |
</threat_model>
