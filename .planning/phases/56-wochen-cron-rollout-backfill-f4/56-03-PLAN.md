<objective>
Build the weekly cron `VoluntaryRebookingScheduler` as a 1:1 mirror of `PdfExportSchedulerImpl`, wire it into `main.rs` DI, and boot it at the end of `main()` after `pdf_export_scheduler.start()`. The scheduler computes the last completed ISO week (Vorwoche) and calls `run_for_week` (Plan 02) for every capped SalesPerson.

Purpose: This is the automation surface for REB-AUTO-01. The heavy lifting (excess, idempotency, Locked-skip, toggle-gate) already lives in `run_for_week`; the scheduler is thin orchestration plus the tokio-cron-scheduler bootstrap. Following the pdf_export blueprint exactly (per the verified analog) avoids reinventing the JobScheduler lifecycle, the clone_for_job handle trick, and the 6-field cron syntax that the v2.3.1 hotfix proved mandatory.

Output: scheduler trait + impl (1:1 pdf_export), main.rs DI wiring + boot, a cron seed decision (hardcoded 6-field `0 15 3 * * 1` Monday 03:15, conflict-free vs pdf_export Monday 06:00 per D-03), and one integration test proving run_once_now delegates to run_for_week for the computed Vorwoche.
</objective>
