<objective>
Expose the F4 backfill engine over HTTP: `POST /admin/rebooking/backfill?from=YYYY-Www&dry_run={true|false}` with `BackfillRequestTO` / `BackfillPreviewTO`, HR-Admin gated, dry-run preview vs apply, from-week validation, and pagination for multi-year backfills (per D-05).

Purpose: REB-AUTO-04 requires a REST backfill (no CLI) so HR-Admin can trigger a one-time historical rollout with a safe dry-run preview. The handler is a thin delegate to `backfill_range` (Plan 02); its job is auth enforcement, input validation, and DTO mapping. The threat surface (authz bypass, malformed range, resource exhaustion, zero-write dry-run guarantee) is concentrated here, so it carries the phase's primary externally-reachable threat model.

Output: request/response TOs with ToSchema + OpenAPI, the `trigger_backfill` handler, the `/admin/rebooking` route registration, ApiDoc entry, and an integration test covering dry-run/apply/403/400.
</objective>
